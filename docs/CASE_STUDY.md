# Case study

Residential energy decisions combine technical characteristics, incomplete
documentation, uncertain costs and changing regulatory context.

EnergyScan introduced a staged workflow:

1. capture the property context;
2. validate and normalise the information;
3. evaluate traceable categories;
4. expose confidence and missing evidence;
5. compare improvement scenarios;
6. generate an orientative Premium report;
7. enable a controlled provider handoff.

The project demonstrates how a complex residential-energy journey can become a
structured, understandable and commercially viable digital product.
