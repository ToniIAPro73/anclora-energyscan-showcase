# Portfolio notice

This repository is a reduced public case study of Anclora EnergyScan.

The complete product is maintained privately. This public version excludes:

- application source code;
- energy-scoring formulas and thresholds;
- Catastro integration;
- OCR and vision-analysis implementation;
- Premium PDF generation;
- Stripe checkout and webhook logic;
- provider, partner and lead workflows;
- authentication and authorisation;
- database schemas and migrations;
- tests, credentials and infrastructure.

All examples are conceptual or synthetic. EnergyScan does not issue an official
Energy Performance Certificate or any document with administrative validity.
